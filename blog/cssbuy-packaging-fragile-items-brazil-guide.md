## Practical packaging tips to reduce damage risk

Pack fragile items individually with cushioning that prevents movement. Use double-box packing when possible: an inner box for the product and an outer shipping box with at least 2–3 cm of filler (foam, bubble wrap, or packing peanuts) on all sides. Label boxes as fragile and orient arrows if required.

If items are high value or especially delicate, request stronger packaging and professional packing from the seller or ask CSSBuy about additional packing services and insurance. Keep photos of the packed item and save QC photos provided by CSSBuy for claims or disputes. Remember to confirm available packing services and charges at checkout or with CSSBuy support.
